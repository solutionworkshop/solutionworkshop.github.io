Read the commandments
  1. Thou shalt keep thy passwords in a company vault.
  2. Thou shalt number thy accounts.
  3. Thou shalt set a second guard upon thy gates.
  4. Thou shalt guard thy telephone number from thieves.
  5. Thou shalt render unto the company that which belongeth to the company.
  6. Thou shalt not share thy login with thy neighbor.
  7. Thou shalt preserve the means of recovery.
  8. Thou shalt replace the secrets that have been revealed.
  9. Thou shalt secure the devices entrusted unto thee.
  10. Thou shalt close the gates no longer needed.
